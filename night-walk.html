      This scene is GPU heavy

      Night Walk renders a real-time 3D street in your browser with reflections, rain, bloom and depth of field. It can use a lot of your graphics card while it runs.

      • Older or integrated graphics and some phones may run slowly or get warm
      • On a laptop, it drains the battery faster. Plugging in helps
      • The power button in the corner stops the scene and frees the GPU at any time
